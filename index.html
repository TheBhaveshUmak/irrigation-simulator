Soil-moisture irrigation simulator

We built this to test our irrigation idea before building anything real. It runs our sensor-and-solar system next to an ordinary fixed-schedule timer, with exactly the same weather, so you can see how much water each one uses and whether the crops still do OK. The numbers come from our own simple model, so use them as a guide, not as proof. Tap a patch of soil in the field to water it by hand.

What the prototype would cost

Assumptions you can change: pump 25 W moving 100 L/h, four zones of 10 m2. Both systems use the same seed unless you pick drought-tolerant, and both lose water in the pipes (drip 90% efficient, flood 70%). The savings are what our model predicts. We will measure the real number when the prototype is built.
